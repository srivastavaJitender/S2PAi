Business case for approval
Impact, process comparison, and phased rollout plan — for mixed audience review
⏰
Manual review time eliminated
AP team currently spends ~3 hours/day manually checking tax and WHT codes before Coupa submission. The agent handles every invoice instantly and automatically, 24/7.
~750 hrs / year saved
🛡
SAP integration failures prevented
Each SAP rejection caused by a tax or WHT error requires ~4 hours of rework across AP, finance, and the vendor. The agent catches all errors before they reach SAP.
Zero SAP rejections
🔄
Faster invoice cycle time
Invoices that pass all checks are auto-submitted within 10 minutes of arrival in Coupa — no waiting for AP team availability. Vendor payment timelines improve significantly.
10 min vs 24–48 hrs
👁
Full compliance audit trail
Every agent decision is logged with timestamp, validation results, and Claude AI reasoning. Complete traceability for internal audit, compliance, and management reporting.
100% auditable
