The problem we are solving
🔒
Invoices stuck in Coupa
Invoices with tax or WHT errors are placed on hold in Coupa and sit in a queue. The AP team must manually review each one, identify the error, contact the supplier, and re-submit — often taking 24–48 hours per invoice.
Today: Human-in-the-loop (HITL) — every stuck invoice requires AP team intervention before it can move forward.
⚠
SAP integration failures
Invoices that pass Coupa but carry invalid tax codes or WHT mismatches fail during SAP integration. These generate SAP error queues, require manual correction by finance teams, delay vendor payments, and create compliance risk.
Impact: Each SAP rejection costs ~4 hours of rework across AP, finance, and the vendor — and disrupts payment timelines.
🕐
Manual validation — no scale
The AP team manually checks tax codes, WHT codes, and rates for every invoice before submission. This is repetitive, error-prone, and consumes approximately 3 hours of AP team time per day — time that should be spent on higher-value work.
Root cause: No automated pre-validation layer between invoice submission and the SAP integration pipeline.
😖

Claude agentic AI — business benefits
Why this is the right solution for EP&P Ops
⏰
Eliminates HITL on clean invoices
The AP team no longer needs to manually review every invoice before submission. Claude validates autonomously — human effort is only needed when the agent flags a genuine error that the supplier must correct.
Estimated saving to be confirmed with EP&P Ops team
🛡
Prevents SAP integration failures
Every tax and WHT error is caught before the invoice reaches the SAP integration layer. This eliminates SAP error queues, prevents payment delays, and removes the rework cycle that currently costs ~4 hours per rejection.
Zero SAP rejections
🧠
Smarter than HITL over time
Unlike manual review, Claude remembers every vendor's history. A vendor that repeatedly submits wrong WHT codes gets escalated — not just held again. The agent detects systemic issues that humans in a manual process would miss across hundreds of invoices.
Learns every invoice
💬
Actionable error comments in Coupa
Claude writes its own contextual comment directly in the Coupa invoice — not a generic error code. The supplier sees exactly which line, which code, and what to fix. First-time fix rate improves significantly vs today's manual process.
Specific & actionable
↻
Auto re-validates after correction
Once the supplier corrects the invoice in Coupa, the agent detects the change within 10 minutes and re-runs validation automatically. No AP team action needed to re-trigger the process — the HITL loop is removed entirely.
10 min re-validation
👁
Full audit trail — zero blind spots
Every agent decision is logged with Claude's full reasoning, tool chain, and iteration count. AP managers and auditors can see exactly what was checked, what was found, and why the decision was made — with no gaps or undocumented manual steps.
100% traceable
